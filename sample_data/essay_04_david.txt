Title: Reducing State Space via Ample Sets

When modeling multi-threaded distributed protocols, an exponential state space explosion proportional to 2^n occurs across n executing threads. Our project investigated partial order reduction algorithms based on independent transition commutativity.

We implemented an ample set construction method for stuttering-free properties. In our test benchmarks, this technique reduced visited states by an average factor of 3.4x without losing soundness across all checked safety assertions.
