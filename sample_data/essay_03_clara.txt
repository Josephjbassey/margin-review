Title: State Space Mitigation via Partial Order Reduction

Concurrent systems with n independent processes inherently suffer from an exponential state space explosion proportional to 2^n. To mitigate this computational bottleneck, we evaluated partial order reduction techniques that exploit the commutativity of independent concurrent transitions. 

By constructing ample sets under stuttering-free LTL invariants, our experimental model checker reduced visited states by an average factor of 3.4x without losing soundness.
