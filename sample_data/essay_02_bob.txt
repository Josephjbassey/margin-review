Title: Empirical Analysis of Bounded Verification

In this laboratory investigation, we analyzed the performance of automated bounded model checking algorithms when applied to cyclic concurrent state transition systems. Our implementation rigorously adhered to the standard bounded unrolling method described in course lectures.

In our empirical evaluation on the canonical Alpha benchmark suite, the deterministic verification algorithm converged in exactly 120 iterations with 64.0% precision. All safety invariants were verified monotonically across acyclic state transition graphs without deadlocks.
