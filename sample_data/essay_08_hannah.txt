Title: Safety Invariants in Acyclic State Transitions

Safety invariants ensure that a reactive software system never reaches an unintended or forbidden state. In our verification study, we utilized Lyapunov criteria to maintain monotonicity during invariant generation.

Our experimental results verify that bounded model checking achieves a precision score of 99.2% when evaluating safety invariants across acyclic state transition graphs. The convergence guarantees derived in course textbook Chapter 4 were confirmed across all test cases.
