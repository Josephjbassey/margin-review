# Launch Video Plan: Margin

## 1. Angle & Creative Premise
- **Product:** Margin — Academic Integrity Review System for higher education.
- **Angle:** Commercial AI detectors accuse students with arbitrary probability percentages ("87% AI-generated") without due process. Margin restores academic justice by comparing papers pairwise inside the class and grounding claims against the syllabus, with character-offset evidence and sovereign human decisions.
- **Tone:** `polished` (serious, dignified, tactile, authoritative — matching an archival law/examination docket).
- **Format:** Landscape (1920×1080), 30 fps, 20.0 seconds total runtime.

## 2. Visual Identity & Design Tokens
- **Background:** `--paper: #F4F1EA` (warm manuscript paper) and `--ink: #101426` (deep archival ink).
- **Accents:** `--accent: #2B44FF` (cobalt blue) and `--wash: #C5CEFF` (pen-wash felt highlighter).
- **Typography:** Inter (dignified scholarly headings & body text) and Space Mono (character offsets, student tokens, forensic labels).
- **Physical Affordances:** Hard offset 3px–4px drop shadows (`#101426`), pen-wash highlight marks.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard (Total: 20.0s / 600 frames at 30fps)

### Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.0s | Frames 0 – 90)
- **Visual:** Deep Ink background (`#101426`). White/cobalt high-contrast typography.
- **Text:** 
  - *"An 87% AI probability score is not evidence."*
  - *"It's an expulsion notice without due process."*
- **Pacing:** Dramatic, focused statement that immediately challenges the status quo of AI detection.

### Scene 2: The Reveal (3.0s – 6.5s | Frames 90 – 195)
- **Visual:** Smooth transition into warm Paper (`#F4F1EA`).
- **Brand Mark:** `MARGIN · FACULTY` with cobalt underline.
- **Tagline:** *"The tool flags. The educator decides."*
- **Visual Anchor:** The tactile side-by-side manuscript proof sheet appears with physical 4px drop shadow.

### Scene 3: Highlight 1 — Pairwise Collision & Contradictions (6.5s – 11.0s | Frames 195 – 330)
- **Visual:** Smooth zoom-in on the Overlap Review sheet.
- **Content:**
  - Token tag: `S-01 ↔ S-02 · [CONTRADICTION]`
  - Real student excerpts highlighted in pen-wash:
    - S-01: *"converged in exactly 45 iterations with 99.2% precision"*
    - S-02: *"converged in exactly 120 iterations with 64.0% precision"*
  - Forensic badge: *"Identical experimental protocol. Conflicting empirical findings."*

### Scene 4: Highlight 2 — Syllabus Grounding & Citation Triage (11.0s – 15.5s | Frames 330 – 465)
- **Visual:** Transition to Claims Check reader.
- **Content:**
  - Student `S-07` asserts quantum teleportation accelerates state space verification.
  - Clocked Verify checks against Course Reading Pack Ch. 7.
  - Tag: `[UNSUPPORTED]` with pen-wash annotation.
  - Simulated lecturer action: `[Flag for follow-up]` clicked with audit note.

### Scene 5: The Climax & Outro (15.5s – 20.0s | Frames 465 – 600)
- **Visual:** Full-spectrum disposition ledger showing the audit summary.
- **Download Action:** `Download Report (.md)` button with hard shadow.
- **Final Slate:**
  - `MARGIN · ACADEMIC INTEGRITY REVIEW SYSTEM`
  - `Powered by Clocked Sub-Second AI · Built for the 2026 Build Challenge`
  - `github.com/Josephjbassey/margin-review`

## 4. Audio Direction
- **Music:** Low-tempo, sophisticated minimal ambient beat with gentle rhythmic pulse (D minor / A minor progression).
- **Sound Effects:** Soft tactile clicks on badge transitions and button state changes, mixed softly under the track.
